# Business Central Card Page IDs

> **Purpose:** Lookup table of standard Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central record-level pages and their Page IDs, used by the GetURL v3 API to build bookmark and deep-link URLs to a specific record's card page.
>
> **Scope:** Every page with `PageType = Card`, `Document`, or `ListPlus` in the Microsoft **Base Application**, **System Application**, and **Business Foundation** apps that is a genuine record deep-link target, organized into one collapsible section per page type. Statistics popups, setup pages, action dialogs, analysis views, admin tools, and NA-localization-only objects are excluded. Extracted from the official symbol packages for **BC version 28.1** (Base Application 28.1.49838.50974).
>
> **Generated:** July 8, 2026 from SymbolReference.json in the .app symbol packages.
