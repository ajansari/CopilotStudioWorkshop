# Contoso USA Employee Handbook
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Location:** Houston, Texas  
**Industry:** Manufacturing and Distribution of Office Equipment

---

## 1. Welcome to Contoso USA

Welcome to Contoso USA. This handbook outlines key policies, expectations, and benefits.

This handbook is not a contract. Employment is **at-will** and may be terminated at any time by either party.

---

## 2. Company Overview

### Mission
Deliver reliable office equipment through operational excellence and innovation.

### Core Values
- Integrity  
- Safety  
- Quality  
- Customer Focus  
- Continuous Improvement  

---

## 3. Employment Basics

### Equal Employment Opportunity
We do not discriminate based on protected characteristics under federal or Texas law.

### Employment Classification
- Full-time (30+ hrs/week)
- Part-time (<30 hrs/week)
- Temporary
- Exempt / Non-exempt

---

## 4. Work Schedule & Attendance

### Standard Hours
- Manufacturing: Shift-based (e.g., 6am–2pm / 2pm–10pm)
- Office staff: Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm

### Attendance Policy
- More than **3 unexcused absences in 90 days** may trigger disciplinary action
- Employees must notify supervisors at least 1 hour before shift start

---

## 5. Compensation

### Pay
- Paid **biweekly**
- Direct deposit is required

### Overtime
- Non-exempt employees earn **1.5x pay** after 40 hours/week

---

## 6. Time Off & Leave

### Paid Time Off (PTO)

| Years of Service | Annual PTO |
|-----------------|-----------|
| 0–1 years       | 10 days   |
| 1–5 years       | 15 days   |
| 5+ years        | 20 days   |

- PTO accrues per pay period
- Max carryover: **5 days into next year**

### Holidays (Paid)
- New Year’s Day  
- Memorial Day  
- Independence Day  
- Labor Day  
- Thanksgiving (2 days)  
- Christmas Day  

### Sick Leave
- 5 days per year (included in PTO bucket or separate if preferred)

### Unpaid Leave
- FMLA (if eligible)
- Military leave
- Jury duty

---

## 7. Benefits

Eligible employees (full-time) receive:
- Medical, dental, vision insurance
- 401(k) with company match (up to 4%)
- Life insurance (1x annual salary)
- Short-term disability

---

## 8. Workplace Conduct

Employees must:
- Treat coworkers respectfully  
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or retaliation  
- Maintain professional behavior  

Zero tolerance for:
- Violence or threats  
- Substance abuse on company property  
- Theft or fraud  

---

## 9. Social Media Policy

Employees may use social media freely but must follow these guidelines:

### Do:
- Be respectful and professional  
- Clearly state opinions are personal  
- Protect company reputation  

### Do NOT:
- Share confidential company information  
- Post offensive, discriminatory, or threatening content  
- Represent Contoso USA without authorization  

Violations may result in disciplinary action, up to termination.

---

## 10. Technology & Acceptable Use

### Acceptable Use
- Company devices are for business purposes
- Limited personal use allowed if it does not interfere with work

### Prohibited Use
- Accessing illegal or inappropriate content  
- Installing unauthorized software  
- Sharing login credentials  

All activity may be monitored.

---

## 11. Safety (Manufacturing-Specific)

### General Safety Rules
- PPE required (e.g., gloves, safety glasses)
- Report incidents immediately

### Equipment Operation
- Training required before use  
- Follow all operating procedures  

### Lockout/Tagout
- Required during maintenance  
- Only authorized personnel may perform procedures  

### Forklift Safety
- Certification required  
- Speed limits enforced in warehouse  

---

## 12. Drug & Alcohol Policy

- Substance use is prohibited during work hours  
- Random drug testing may be conducted in safety-sensitive roles  
- Violations may result in immediate termination  

---

## 13. Workplace Violence Policy

Contoso USA has zero tolerance for:
- Threats or intimidation  
- Physical violence  
- Weapons on company property  

Report concerns immediately to HR or management.

---

## 14. Performance Management

### Reviews
- Annual performance reviews  
- Mid-year check-ins

### Improvement Plans
Employees may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) if needed.

---

## 15. Discipline Policy

Progressive discipline may include:
1. Verbal warning  
2. Written warning  
3. Final warning or suspension  
4. Termination  

Serious violations may skip steps.

---

## 16. Separation & Final Pay

### Voluntary Resignation
- 2 weeks’ notice expected

### Final Pay
- Provided per Texas law timelines

### Return of Property
- All company equipment must be returned

---

## 17. Confidentiality

Employees must:
- Protect customer, vendor, and company data  
- Not disclose proprietary information  

Applies during and after employment.

## 18. Policy Changes

Contoso USA may update policies at any time. Employees will be notified of material changes.
