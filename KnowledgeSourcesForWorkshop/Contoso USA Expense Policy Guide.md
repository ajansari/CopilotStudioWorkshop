# Contoso USA Expense Policy Guide

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** June 25, 2026  
**Applies To:** All Contoso USA employees, contractors (if contractually eligible), and managers involved in expense submission or approval

---

## 1. Purpose

This Expense Policy Guide defines the rules for submitting, approving, and reimbursing business expenses at **Contoso USA**. The goal is to ensure that expenses are:

- Business-related
- Reasonable and necessary
- Properly documented
- Approved through the correct workflow
- Compliant with internal controls

---

## 2. General Policy

All reimbursable expenses must meet the following requirements:

- Must be incurred for a legitimate business purpose
- Must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the expense date
- Must include **itemized receipts**
- Must comply with spending limits and approval requirements in this guide
- Must be submitted through the company’s approved expense management process

**Important Rule:**  
**All expenses must have receipts.** Any expense submitted without a receipt will be rejected unless a documented exception is approved by the employee’s manager and Finance.

---

## 3. Receipt Requirements

A valid receipt should include:

- Vendor name
- Date of purchase
- Items or services purchased
- Total amount paid
- Proof of payment, where applicable

### Missing Receipt Exceptions
If a receipt is missing, the employee must:

1. Attempt to obtain a duplicate receipt from the vendor
2. Submit a written explanation
3. Obtain manager approval
4. Receive Finance review before reimbursement can be considered

Contoso USA reserves the right to deny any expense lacking adequate documentation.

---

## 4. Reimbursable Expense Categories

The following categories are generally reimbursable when incurred for approved business purposes:

### Travel
- Airfare
- Hotel/lodging
- Ground transportation
- Mileage for personal vehicle use
- Parking and tolls

### Meals
- Individual business travel meals
- Client meals, when business purpose and attendees are documented

### Office and Business Supplies
- Small office supplies
- Printing or shipping for business use

### Training and Events
- Registration fees for approved conferences, training, and seminars

---

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursable unless pre-approved in writing by Finance and a department leader:

- Personal entertainment
- Alcohol, unless part of an approved client event
- Personal travel extensions or upgrades
- Spouse, family, or guest travel costs
- Traffic or parking fines
- Late payment fees
- Lost or stolen personal property
- Luxury or premium services not required for business

---

## 6. Hotel Expense Rule

Hotel expenses must be reasonable and aligned with business needs.

### Standard Rule
- **Hotel receipts must not exceed $100 per day**

### Exception Rule
- If a hotel receipt **exceeds $100 per day**, the expense requires:
  - **Manager approval**, and
  - A clear business justification in the expense report

### Examples of Acceptable Justifications
- Conference hotel block with no lower-cost option available
- Travel to a high-cost city during peak demand
- Safety or client-location considerations
- Last-minute travel approved by leadership

### What Happens If This Rule Is Not Followed
- Hotel expenses over $100/day without manager approval may be:
  - Sent back for correction
  - Partially reimbursed
  - Rejected entirely

---

## 7. Meal Guidelines

### While Traveling
Employees may submit reasonable meal expenses incurred during approved business travel.

### Required Documentation
Meal receipts must include:
- Itemized receipt
- Business purpose
- Names of attendees, if more than one person is included

### Limits
Managers should review meals for reasonableness. Excessive meal claims may be reduced or denied.

---

## 8. Transportation Guidelines

### Air Travel
- Economy class is the standard
- Business or first class requires prior approval from a department director

### Personal Vehicle Mileage
- Reimbursed at the current Contoso USA approved mileage rate
- Mileage logs must include:
  - Date
  - Starting point
  - Destination
  - Business purpose
  - Total miles

### Rideshare / Taxi
- Allowed when reasonable and necessary
- Receipt required

---

## 9. Expense Submission Deadlines

Employees must submit expenses within:

- **30 calendar days** from the date the expense was incurred
- **10 business days** after completion of business travel, whichever comes first in practice

Expenses submitted late may require additional approval and may be denied.

## 10. Fictitious Approval Workflow

Below is the **Contoso USA standard expense approval workflow**.

## Step 1: Employee Submission
The employee:

- Enters the expense in the expense system
- Attaches all required receipts
- Adds a business purpose for each item
- Identifies any exceptions, such as hotel charges over $100/day

## Step 2: Direct Manager Review
The employee’s **Direct Manager** reviews the report for:

- Business purpose
- Policy compliance
- Receipt completeness
- Reasonableness of cost
- Any exceptions requiring explanation

The manager may:

- Approve
- Reject
- Return for edits

## Step 3: Department Budget Owner Approval
If the total report is **greater than $500**, the expense also routes to the **Department Budget Owner** for budget validation.

The Budget Owner reviews:

- Department budget availability
- Alignment to approved business activities
- Any unusual or out-of-policy spend

## Step 4: Finance Audit Review
All approved expense reports route to **Finance Operations** for final review.

Finance checks:

- Receipt documentation
- Math accuracy
- Duplicate submissions
- Policy compliance
- Tax handling
- Proper approvals for exceptions

Finance may:
- Approve for payment
- Return to employee
- Escalate to HR or Leadership for suspected misuse

## Step 5: Payment Processing
Once Finance approves the report:

- Reimbursement is scheduled in the next available payment cycle
- Typical reimbursement target is **7–10 business days**

## 11. Exception Approval Matrix

| Scenario | Required Approval |
|---|---|
| Standard expense with valid receipt | Direct Manager |
| Hotel expense over $100/day | Direct Manager |
| Total expense report over $500 | Direct Manager + Department Budget Owner |
| Missing receipt | Direct Manager + Finance |
| Airfare upgrade | Department Director |
| Late submission | Direct Manager + Finance |
| Unusual or out-of-policy expense | Direct Manager + Finance, possibly Director |

## 12. Roles and Responsibilities

### Employee
Employees are responsible for:

- Following this policy
- Keeping receipts
- Submitting expenses on time
- Providing truthful and complete information
- Requesting approval when exceptions are needed

### Direct Manager
Managers are responsible for:

- Reviewing expense reports promptly
- Confirming business purpose
- Ensuring policy compliance
- Approving exceptions only when justified
- Escalating questionable claims

### Department Budget Owner
Budget Owners are responsible for:

- Confirming available budget
- Reviewing large or unusual expense reports
- Ensuring spending aligns with departmental priorities

### Finance Operations
Finance is responsible for:

- Final policy review
- Audit checks
- Reimbursement processing
- Record retention
- Escalation of non-compliance issues

---

## 13. Policy Violations

The following may be treated as policy violations:

- Submitting expenses without receipts
- Repeated late submissions
- Misrepresenting business purpose
- Splitting expenses to avoid approval thresholds
- Submitting personal expenses as business expenses
- Altering receipts or documentation

Violations may result in:

- Expense denial
- Repayment obligation
- Manager counseling
- Disciplinary action, up to and including termination

## 14. Practical Examples

### Example 1: Hotel Under Limit
An employee stays one night for a client meeting and submits a hotel receipt for **$92**.  
- Receipt attached  
- Business purpose included  
- Manager approves  
- Finance processes reimbursement

### Example 2: Hotel Over Limit
An employee attends a conference and submits a hotel receipt for **$165/day**.  
- Receipt attached  
- Employee notes that the conference hotel was required  
- Manager approves exception  
- Finance reviews and reimburses

### Example 3: Missing Receipt
An employee submits a taxi expense but has no receipt.  
- Employee submits written explanation  
- Manager approves  
- Finance reviews  
- Expense may still be denied if documentation is insufficient
